Executive Summary

The subject property is a 5,714 sq.ft commercial/residential plot located in the established and highly accessible Al Hudaibah district of Ras Al Khaimah. It sits in a mature, central neighbourhood offering excellent connectivity to key city infrastructure and proximity to primary road arteries.

Permitted development allows for Ground + 1 Floor (G+1) with a maximum 50% BUA footprint per level, yielding a total GFA of 5,714 sq.ft. The current natural-surface secondary access represents a classic value-add opportunity — the plot can be acquired below peak rates, with built-in appreciation once the municipality finalises road paving.

Study 01 — Neighbourhood / Secondary Market Context

Commercial and mixed-use land within central RAK districts commands a strong premium due to scarcity and high rental demand. Active comparable listings in the immediate micro-market currently span AED 143–200 per sqft, with main-road positioning commanding the top of the range.

The subject plot's natural-surface secondary access positions it below the AED 180–200 peak tier, creating a clear discount-to-value entry point ahead of infrastructure formalisation.
