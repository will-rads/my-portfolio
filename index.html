01
Brochure pipeline

Sheet row in, live site out

Scheduled n8n workflow, 29 nodes. Reads ready rows from a sheet of land plots, pulls the Drive images, runs two Gemini passes to turn a row into a branded HTML page, deploys it to Vercel, and generates a Rebrandly link. Writes the link back to the sheet and marks the row done. No manual steps.

04
Meta CAPI

Rebuilt after the tracking broke

Leads arrive as Click-to-WhatsApp ads. A WhatsApp Cloud API webhook feeds n8n, which keeps only ad-originated messages, dedups them, hashes phone numbers, drops stale events, and posts server-side Purchase events to Meta's Conversions API. Ad sets are managed through the Marketing API. Rebuilt from scratch after a provider change broke attribution.
